How it works

SqueekShot is a prediction game powered by Squeekchain, the blockchain whose blocks are produced by Squeektoshi, a real hamster on a real wheel. Every few seconds this page reads the chain's public telemetry (hamster state and wheel RPM from api.squeekchain.com) and settles your calls against what the hamster actually does next.

While he runs: call the peak RPM of the next 20 seconds against the current RPM — Drops, Steady, Surge or Rocket. While he sleeps: call whether he wakes within 60 seconds. Harder calls pay more. Win streaks add a bonus of +10% per streak step (max +100%). Scores live in your browser — no wallet, no keys, nothing to sign.
